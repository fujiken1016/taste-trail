Taste Trail

Tell us what you love. We build a 7-day Japanese immersion trail from media that matches your taste, and check each pick against your level.

Getting ready. Live Qloo data is being connected to this demo, so it is switched off for now rather than showing made-up results. The code is open source: github.com/fujiken1016/taste-trail
Tip: add (game), (show), (film), (music) or (book) after a name if it is ambiguous, e.g. Hades (game).

Qloo results are aggregate taste affinities, not facts about you. The level rule is our own heuristic, shown separately.
